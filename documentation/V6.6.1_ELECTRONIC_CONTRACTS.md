# v6.6.1 — Electronic Contract Acceptance

Client flow:
1. Review the generated maintenance/hosting agreement.
2. Enter full legal name.
3. Confirm the agreement and recurring billing.
4. Select Sign & Accept Agreement.
5. Continue automatically to Stripe Checkout.

The backend stores the exact agreement snapshot, SHA-256 hash, typed legal name,
acceptance timestamp, request IP address and user-agent. Stripe setup is blocked
until the agreement has been accepted.

Accepted snapshots are retained on the recurring contract. Material term changes
should be issued as a new agreement/version rather than overwriting an accepted one.
