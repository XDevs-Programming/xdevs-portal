Privacy Policy

This policy explains how XDevs Programming collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you use the website, client portal or commission services.

1. Who we are

XDevs Programming operates the XDevs Programming website and client portal. For personal information processed through these services, XDevs Programming is responsible for deciding why and how that information is used.

Privacy enquiries and data-rights requests can be made through .

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the portal, we may process:

  • Account information: username, email address, profile image and identifiers supplied by Google or Discord when you choose to sign in through those services.
  • Commission information: project titles, descriptions, requested features, budgets, deadlines, status, notes and completion details.
  • Communication information: messages sent through commission live chat, reviews and other information you choose to submit.
  • Files: project files, references, deliverables and associated metadata such as filename, type, size, uploader and upload time.
  • Payment and invoice information: payment status, amount, invoice number, billing description and Stripe transaction references. XDevs Programming does not store your full payment-card details.
  • Technical information: authentication/session information, browser storage used by the portal, security logs and basic request information needed to run and protect the service.
  • Notification preferences: whether you allow browser notifications and notification read/unread state inside the portal.

3. Why we use your information

We use personal information only where there is an appropriate lawful basis. Depending on the activity, this may include:

  • Contract: to create and manage your account, respond to commission requests, deliver work, provide project chat, issue invoices and manage payments.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate, secure, improve and troubleshoot the portal, prevent abuse, maintain records and understand how the service is being used where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where records must be kept or information must be provided to comply with applicable law, accounting or regulatory requirements.
  • Consent: where we specifically ask for permission, for example browser notification permission. You can withdraw that permission through your browser settings.

4. Who we share information with

We use third-party services to operate the portal. Information is shared only as needed for those services to perform their role.

  • Google and Discord for optional OAuth sign-in.
  • Stripe for secure payment processing and payment-status information.
  • MongoDB Atlas for application database storage.
  • Render for backend/API hosting.
  • Netlify for frontend website hosting.
  • Cloudflare R2 for private project-file storage and signed file transfers.
  • YouTube where a public or client-facing project showcase contains an embedded video.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to establish or defend legal claims, or to protect users, XDevs Programming or others from fraud, abuse or security threats.

5. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards and transfer mechanisms made available by the relevant provider and required by applicable data-protection law.

6. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, including providing commissioned work, maintaining invoice and transaction records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, meeting legal/accounting obligations and protecting the security of the service.

Different categories may therefore have different retention periods. A request to delete an account does not necessarily require us to erase records that we must retain for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes.

7. Security

We use access controls, authenticated API routes, role checks, private object storage, signed file-transfer URLs, HTTPS, OAuth authentication and other technical measures intended to protect portal information.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. You should protect access to your Google or Discord account and report suspected unauthorised portal access promptly.

8. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • ask for access to personal information held about you;
  • ask for inaccurate information to be corrected;
  • ask for information to be erased in certain circumstances;
  • ask us to restrict how information is used;
  • object to certain processing;
  • receive certain information in a portable format; and
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe your data-protection rights have been infringed.

9. Cookies and browser storage

The portal uses browser storage to keep you signed in and remember essential application state. Third-party authentication, payment and embedded-content providers may also use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Payment-card data: card information entered into Stripe Checkout is handled by Stripe rather than being stored in the XDevs Programming application database.

10. Browser notifications

If you enable browser notifications, your browser may display alerts for relevant portal activity. Notification permission is controlled by your browser or operating system and can be disabled at any time.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the portal, legal requirements or service providers change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

To ask a privacy question or exercise a data-protection right, contact XDevs Programming through .
